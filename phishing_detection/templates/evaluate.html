Cybersecurity ML Evaluation Rationale: Precision vs. Recall

Precision measures the accuracy of positive phishing flags (minimizing False Positives / legitimate websites incorrectly blocked). Recall measures the coverage of actual threats (minimizing False Negatives / missed phishing attacks). An ideal defensive classifier maximizes the F1-Score (harmonic mean of Precision and Recall).
